# Data contract

The research target is Oxford-IIIT Pet: 7,349 images and 37 classes. Acquisition remains
locked until license, checksums and canonical split provenance are reviewed. Train is used for
optimization and PTQ calibration, validation for decisions, and test stays locked until the
configuration freezes.

The bundled 12-image generated corpus is only a qualification fixture. Its IDs, labels,
splits and SHA-256 checksums validate disjointness, APIs and UI states without masquerading as
Oxford-IIIT Pet evidence.
