# Demo script

1. Open **Overview** and state: procedural qualification, not KSDD2.
2. Open **Inspect**, choose a defective showcase surface and run Small U-Net.
3. Compare source, probability, binary mask and overlay; show area and components.
4. Move the threshold and show that inference and the decision update for real.
5. Compare U-Net with the OpenCV morphology baseline.
6. Explain validation-only selection versus piece policy in **Threshold lab**.
7. Contrast pixel metrics and false-accept/false-reject behavior in **Evaluation**.
8. Explain in **Errors** that zero failures on 12 procedural images is not generalization.
9. Finish on **Model record** with checkpoint hash and the locked official test.
