# Threats to validity

- Four generated receipts cannot represent production layout, language, scan or camera drift.
- SROIE was not acquired; no official split or score is claimed.
- Fixture boxes are aligned by construction and overstate real localization quality.
- The layout extractor uses a narrow bilingual lexicon and four fixed business fields.
- The optional PaddleOCR adapter has not been qualified in the lightweight release image.
- PDF rasterization, multipage input, handwriting and tables are outside scope.
- Confidence is a policy score, not a calibrated probability of correctness.
- The in-memory extraction store is suitable for demonstration, not durable audit history.
- AWS deployment assets are release-ready only after authenticated validation and smoke tests.
